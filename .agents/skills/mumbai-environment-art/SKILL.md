name: mumbai-environment-art
description: Original CST–Fort first-person environment art acceptance guidance.
---

Review streets at the actual pedestrian camera before approving expansion. Aerial density, test success and code complexity do not establish visual quality.

Buildings require genuine openings/reveals, distinct architecture, restrained surface aging, appropriate scale, contextual street-level function and a finished rooftop. Four walls and attached dark windows are insufficient. Compose foreground objects, midground architecture and background depth; place signs, utilities, pedestrians, vehicles and furniture according to use, not random scatter.

Close humans require authored anatomy/clothing silhouettes, a rig, natural movement and crowd awareness. Primitive mannequins are not final art. Cars require shaped bodywork, cabins, wheels, mirrors and interiors; cuboid cars are not accepted. Obvious back-and-forth loops are not final city behavior.

Keep materials natural, shadows cool and shop light warm. Avoid neon, extreme saturation, heavy vignette and purple sky gradients. Compare the same camera/time/exposure before and after. Document the three worst remaining defects; a generous numerical score cannot override an obvious Roblox-like result.

Keep CST–Fort as the only physical expansion target until visual review and measured laptop performance pass. Preserve accessible portfolio content, physics, interaction and rides throughout. All external assets need source, license, SHA-256 and modification records. This original local skill is advisory and grants no shell, credential, deployment or external-service authority.
