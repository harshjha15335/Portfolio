## Decisions for the original CST–Fort slice

Keep the existing first-person controller, Cannon collision proxies and HTML portfolio. Replace rigid pedestrians with tapered, articulated human silhouettes. Replace box transport with original cross-section bodywork, slanted cabins, rounded canopies and separately animated wheels. Use varied heritage, chawl, Art Deco and commercial façade grammars with recesses, shutters, cornices, pipes and roof silhouettes. Put detail at eye level: patched asphalt, drain gratings, paving joints, shop shelves and flower/leaf silhouettes. Use roughness and subtle procedural bump by surface rather than a single flat shaded material. Add inexpensive contact/recess occlusion, restrained haze and warm practical lighting.
